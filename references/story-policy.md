# Story and translation policy

## Source integrity

- Prefer a primary classical text or an authoritative institutional edition.
- Record the passage, work, chapter, URL, access date, and which beats it supports.
- Describe an anecdote as “recorded in” the source; do not present literary anecdotes as independently verified historical events.
- Mark exact or closely translated source events as `sourced`, reasonable visual bridges as `inferred`, and invented speech or action as `dramatized`.

## Six-beat arc

1. `hook` — reveal the paradox or consequence within 1–2 seconds.
2. `setup` — establish the people, desire, and object at stake.
3. `cause` — introduce the rule, decision, or mistake.
4. `turn` — show the irreversible overreach or discovery.
5. `consequence` — make the loss or result unmistakable.
6. `meaning` — name the idiom and connect it to modern life.

Aim for 50–65 seconds. Let dialogue carry emotion and narration carry context. A visible or audible change should occur every 2–4 seconds even when one illustration stays on screen.

## English adaptation

- Write for a curious general viewer aged roughly 13 and above.
- Prefer short clauses, active voice, familiar verbs, and contractions when natural.
- Translate intent before syntax. Avoid faux-archaic English such as “thou,” “thereupon,” or “henceforth.”
- Explain the practical meaning in one sentence. Do not reduce an idiom to its literal gloss.
- Give the Chinese form and standard Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks on the meaning card.
- Label English analogies as approximate: “A similar English expression is…”. Never claim perfect equivalence without evidence.

## Hook patterns

Use one:

- consequence question: “He finished first—so why did he lose?”
- contradiction: “Doing more cost him everything.”
- visual puzzle: “What is wrong with this snake?”

Do not begin with a book title, dynasty date, channel logo, or greeting.

## Meaning card

Include, in this order:

1. Chinese idiom;
2. pinyin;
3. concise English meaning;
4. optional viewer question.

Example structure:

```text
画蛇添足 · Huà shé tiān zú
Don't ruin good work by overdoing it.
Have you ever done this?
```
