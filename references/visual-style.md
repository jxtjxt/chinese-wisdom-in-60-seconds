# Required `sunlit-storybook-9x16` visual lock

Use one simplified, graphic, semi-abstract 2D cartoon style for every scene and the cover.

## Cartoon rendering

- Build characters from a few large geometric shapes, exaggerated silhouettes, flat matte gouache color blocks, and minimal expressive facial marks.
- Identify recurring characters by silhouette, proportions, two or three color blocks, posture, and one distinctive marking or prop.
- Use a restrained palette led by creamy paper, powder blue, soft orange, golden yellow, and small blue-green accents.
- Reduce each background to a few loose brush masses that communicate place and action without competing with the characters.
- Use simple block shadows. Keep generated art text-free and composite all text later.

## Reject realism

Reject and regenerate any image with realistic animal anatomy, individually rendered fur, lifelike eyes or noses, detailed muscles, cinematic volumetric light, depth of field, dense foliage, complex perspective, photographic texture, glossy 3D, anime conventions, or high-detail realistic picture-book rendering. A viewer's first impression must be “graphic cartoon,” not “realistic animal illustration.”

## Mandatory paper edge and safe region

- Fill the `1080x1920` canvas with light warm paper and paint one centered irregular visual island.
- Keep every outer `8–12%` band mostly unpainted paper and all four corners substantially clean.
- Keep essential faces, gestures, props, bubbles, captions, idiom, and pinyin inside `x: 0.10–0.80`, `y: 0.10–0.78`.
- Reserve extra uncluttered space near speaking characters for compact English bubbles.
- Reject straight borders, pasted rectangles, uniform vignettes, blurred ovals, and full-bleed backgrounds.

## Motion preservation

- Source illustrations must retain visible paper on all four sides at scale `1.06` or a `36 px` pan.
- Do not repair a failed edge with a synthetic border.

## Required QA

- Review one contact sheet containing all six source illustrations and the cover.
- Check consistent abstraction level, recurring identity, focus, paper bands, clean corners, and platform-safe content.
- Inspect every bubble, lower caption, cover title, and meaning card at full resolution and phone size.
